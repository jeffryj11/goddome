**By Jeanna’ Mead** Every girl, young and old, has to face her own reflection. Twirl around. Stare it down. Ask herself: **What is the mirror going to say today?** In this crazy world we live in, there is a filter for everything imaginable. We can make ourselves look younger, slimmer, taller—absolutely breathtaking— with a few taps on a screen. Some of us can even do it in real life, using makeup and tools to enhance what God gave us and shape ourselves into something close to “perfect.” But there’s a catch. Makeup wears off. Filters fade away. And the mirror tells the **real** story, not the **reel** story. And deeper still… when we try to change our appearance, we forget that **God sees our hearts.** He sees: - every disguise - every scar we cover - every spot we scrub - every flaw we try to hide And He still whispers: > **“Has anybody told you that you are beautiful? > You might agree if you could see what I see. > Everything about you is incredible.”** Let that sink deep into your soul. He created you exactly how He wanted you. In His eyes, in His hands, you are: - incredible - His masterpiece - His beloved daughter > **“You should have seen Me smile the day that I made you beautiful for Me…”** Do you ever stop and think about that? That when He made you— He *smiled*? He crafted: - the dimple on your cheek - the curve in your lip - the exact shade of your skin - the shape of your eyes - the everything of your everything Fearfully and wonderfully made. Lovingly. Intentionally. Such love makes my heart hum these words, too: > **“If it’s true that beauty lies in the eye of the beholder, > I want my life—and what’s inside—to give Him something to behold. > I want a heart that is captivating. > I want to hear my Father saying, > ‘Has anybody told you that you are beautiful?’”** Scripture says it perfectly: > **“You should clothe yourself with the beauty that comes from within— > the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, > which is so precious to God.”** > *1 Peter 3:4* The beauty of the heart: - doesn’t wear off - doesn’t wash off - doesn’t need filtering - doesn’t need hiding It shines because God intended it to shine. That’s why it’s so important to show the **real** beauty He placed inside us— to show who we truly are: precious, beloved, handmade daughters created one at a time because He knew the world needed us here. > **“Close your eyes > Look inside > Let Me see the you that you’ve been trying to hide > Long ago, I made you so very beautiful > So I ought to know…”** You are beautiful for Him. Without the fluff. Without the filters. Without the world shouting in your ears. Just His voice, your Creator, speaking over you: > **“You are beautiful for Me.”**

Find Jesus, He’s leading you straight to brighter days So keep your head up, keep your hopes high In the best and the bad times Jesus. That is the pure and simple truth of the matter. Only through knowing God, knowing Jesus and having the Holy Spirit in my life can I hold my head up and keep my hopes high because He is the way, the truth and the life. He just is. That is the simplest thing I know and also the most extraordinary, extravagant mind blowing thing I will ever know. This love, this love, this God.. simply amazes me. Cause the night has to happen for the sun to risse I never really gave much thought to that but then I started to really let the truth soak into my soul and realized that is the truth- we need the night, and how I love the night. I love the display of the stars against the black velvet canvas, the way the moon rises up and seems close enough to reach sometimes, and the calmness that sweeps over me when I am outside marveling at the night’s show. I also feel close to God in the night because I can not make out any other conversations since reading lips in the dark is just about impossible and so, when I am outside, I am listening more closely to my Creator and not trying so hard to understand what other people are talking about. Do you ever feel like you listen too much to others and not enough to God? If you do, then go outside by yourself and look up at the sky and be still, be present, be in awe and listen with your heart- your whole heart and then you will hear His voice, whispering to your soul that you just got to hold on because the sun is going to rise and it will be a different kind of beautiful, a different kind of extraordinary. There’s no healed without hurting Isn’t this the truth? It really dawned on me several months ago when I got poison ivy again - this time all over my face and my arms. I had to cancel my appointments and stay home and, as I stared at my reflection in the bathroom mirror, I realized how often I’ve taken my appearance for granted. Soft skin and a pretty face is what I am known for and what I show others and when my face and arms were covered with a rash and blisters, it was anything but pretty and soft. That time it took to heal, though, was just enough time to give me pause to praise God for all the times that I am not hurting, not covered in rashes and not having to avoid others but it did also make me very aware and ashamed of how much I took my body for granted and how little I stopped to thank God for what I have even when it isn’t the best situation. The really hard hurts that takes healing are the ones that hurt the heart, though, and aren’t as visible as rashes and blisters … and those are the ones that I have had to just let go of and let God do His thing and sure enough, in His time and with His grace, those hurts are healing. Everybody is still learning Still…still learning …still learning to let go, still learning to trust God with every little detail, still learning to love anyways, still learning to see the beauty in the ashes, still learning who I am created to be and still learning from my mistakes and for doing things when I know better. Kind of like leaving the garden alone because I know there is poison ivy growing along the ‘good’ things. I think of it this way now… I have to leave some things alone because it is not in my best interest, and it is not my place and when I get into things that I shouldn’t, then I get hurt and I need to be healed. But then again, so is everybody else and we have to remember that- remember to offer the same grace and mercy we want and expect. I often think that God has so much patience with us and that is how He shows love, too, by giving us chance after chance after chance to become more like Jesus and to do this- love others just as He loves us. Oh I know I’m not a saint But I have got some faith And you can borrow mine . That is why I write my heart out, why I share stories and another reason I pray, because I believe that in the sharing, it’s a bit like borrowing or giving a bit of my faith away to whoever needs it then, There are times when God just nudges me to share a piece of a story, a devotional, a post with someone and thy almost always send me a message thanking me for it. As one friend said to me after receiving a post , “When God pricks your heart, the timing is never wrong. There are no coincidences.” Stick with it, no quick fix, you’re gonna make it That mountain looks biggest Right before you start to climb He has you. It really is that simple. I have lots of faith and if you need to, you can borrow some of mine.
