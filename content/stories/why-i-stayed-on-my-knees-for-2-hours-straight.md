This Time It Is Different By Jeanna’ Mead When I sit down to write, it is a ritual, an act of preparation that gets me into the space I need to be in more ways than one. I set down my journal, my bible, a large bottle of iced water and a collection of reading glasses. I open up my hands and just let go, and let the words flow out from my heart through my fingertips and sometimes, I stop and read what I have written and make corrections and other times, I just keep right on going until I am all done, and then I check out the grammar and spelling errors and fix them. This time isn’t different, but what I am about to get into is. Are we afraid to be different? Have we become so afraid of stepping on toes, of hurting feelings, or “butting into other’s business” that we completely miss out on the calling that God has placed upon us as Christians to be transformed and set apart and share the truth - the hard truth with others? I had this image of a teenage girl standing on the edge of a bridge about to jump off and, rather than trying to coax her back from the edge or even grab her and keep her from falling, those that saw her just walked on by, saying things like, “I don’t want to get her parents upset with me,” and “nobody asked my opinion about the situation so I am not going to say anything unless I am asked first,’ and “Oh, once somebody makes it that far, you can’t change their mind.” We have got to be different even when it is difficult, Meanwhile that girl is standing there, silently pleading for someone to care enough, to be brave enough, to love her enough to step up to her and stop her before she makes a choice that can not be taken back, that can lead to death- physical, emotional, spiritual. We are called to be a light in the darkness and right now, the forces of darkness are all around us. We are “minding our business” and letting Satan push children off the cliff, giving Satan the reins to our relatives and friends because we are falling into the “pretty little picture” where everybody and everything is okay until it isn’t, We HAVE to be different NOW! We have to be willing to get our hands dirty, to reach out across the abyss, to step on some toes in order to save lives, and in order to bring these people to God and far away from Satan’s grasp. We can not just sit back and be “ neutral” on things like this. We can not just “ wait and see’ or “ send good vibes” as a way of letting ourselves off the hook. We have to be bold and be of great courage and we have to speak truth with love and grace. We have to be different. We have to dare to be different in Jesus’s name. 

> “This Time It Is Different”
By Jeanna’ Mead
