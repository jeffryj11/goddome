**By Jeanna’ Mead** When I sit down to write, it becomes a kind of ritual — an act of preparation that places me exactly where I need to be, both inside and out. I set down my journal and my Bible. I fill a large bottle with iced water. I gather a collection of reading glasses. Then I open my hands, let go, and let the words flow from my heart through my fingertips. Sometimes I pause to read what I’ve written, fixing pieces along the way. Other times, I just keep going until everything inside me spills out. This time isn’t different — but **what I’m about to get into is.** --- ## **Are we afraid to be different?** Do we avoid stepping on toes? Do we hold back out of fear of hurting feelings? Do we refuse to “butt in” because we don’t want the discomfort — even when God is calling us to speak truth? Have we become so cautious, so timid, that we miss the entire calling to be **transformed**, to be **set apart**, to share **the hard truth** with others? This vision came to me: A teenage girl stands at the edge of a bridge. She is about to jump. People walk past her saying: - *“I don’t want to upset her parents…”* - *“Nobody asked for my opinion, so I’m not going to say anything.”* - *“When someone gets that far, you can’t change their mind.”* Meanwhile, that girl is silently pleading — begging for someone brave enough to care. Someone bold enough to **step in**. Someone loving enough to **stop her** before she makes a decision she can never take back. A decision that could mean death — physical, emotional, spiritual. --- ## **We have GOT to be different.** We are called to be **light in the darkness**, and right now the darkness is loud, bold, and everywhere. While we “mind our own business,” Satan pushes children off cliffs. He tightens his grip on relatives and friends because we prefer the comfort of pretending everything is okay — until it isn’t. **We HAVE to be different now.** We must be willing to: - get our hands dirty - cross the abyss - step on some toes - speak truth - save lives We cannot stay neutral. We cannot whisper “good vibes” as an excuse for inaction. We cannot wait for someone else to step up. --- ## **We must be bold.** We must: - speak truth - with love - with grace - with courage - with urgency We must dare to be different — in the name of Jesus. Because this time... **This time it is different.**

Dear Loved One There is so much I wish I had said to you when I had the chance and so many things that I would do differently if I had only known that I would never get a chance to look you in the eyes again. I would tell you that I love you, quite simply more than you ever realized and that I only wish saying “I love you” had been as easy as all the other things we would say without thinking twice. I wish I had written letters- old fashioned, cursive handwriting on beautiful stationary and tucked into envelopes and stamped in the care of the post office. I wish you had gotten mail from me instead of just bills, and marketing junk that caused you to sigh at the wastefulness. I like to imagine you would have read the letters and smiled and tucked them away, someplace, anywhere but I am not sure. I wish I had tried, at least, so that even if you didn’t smile, you would have read them and known in your heart of hearts that you were loved by me. If I could go back in time, I would reach out more fearlessly, more courageously, more often and I wouldn’t have given up and given in when you kept shutting the doors and ignoring my words. You see, I wish I had been more like Jesus and less like me, more willing to step up and step out of my comfort zone, and more willing to risk having the door slammed in my face because it just might have been opened instead. Jesus left the ninety nine to go after the one and, oh, I wish. I had gone after you, too, and sat with you and talked to you just like Jesus did. Just like He is doing right now in heaven, because I know you are up there because you didn’t go to church but you believed in Jesus and accepted Him long before I even came into your life, disrupting so much of what you knew and wanted and had. Sometimes I just imagine what it will be like when I get up yonder to heaven, and it makes me smile because then I know good and well that you will be jumping up from the table and running over to see me, too, along with all these other people that we have let down and let God take over and get them right where He wants them. That is what I imagine, and I hold on to that because I know that all these regrets and hardships and missed chances will no longer matter up yonder like they do down here. Jesus sure does love us, you know, and I sure wish we had spent some time talking about how great His love for us is, instead of grumbling about how little we felt about other things. I sure wish we had talked and listened to each other a lot more and let bygones be bygones just like Jesus does. You know, He took all those things upon Himself when He died on the cross for us… yea, He was so courageous and loved us so much that He took it all on because He knew we wouldn’t and couldn't ever do anything on our own without His sacrifice . He did it all, all of it. I just wish I had acted a little more like Him and less like me towards you. I know He forgives me and I am trying real hard to forgive myself but somedays it really is hard so that is why I am writing to you now. I am sorry that I let the things of this world take the place of what it was supposed to be on earth as in heaven.I am sorry that I didn’t do my part to be a peace maker like Jesus, I was so intent on keeping the peace,that I let too much time pass by and too many things come up for no good reason. I look back and shake my head because I know I could have done so much better and I would have if only I had tried to be more like Jesus and less like me. It is a real good thing that Jesus is Jesus and I sure am trying to be more and more like Him. I love you. Save me a seat up yonder, okay? Love, Me
