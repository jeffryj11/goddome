A Beautiful Gate Writing my heart out- Jeanna’ Mead “Now a man who was lame from birth was being carried to the temple gate called Beautiful where he was put every day to beg from those going into the temple courts.” (Acts 3:2) I had never noticed this verse, skimming over it as I read the bible and do my devotionals each morning but this time, it seems like the words jumped out at me and tears filled my eyes as I thought about what the word “beautiful” means to me and how different it feels these days. “Look at us!” Here Peter tells the lame man to look up at him and John and that is when I understood so completely- the lame man was embarrassed, even ashamed and rather than making eye contact and giving his full attention, he most likely held his head down, keeping his gaze downcast, and his posture defeated, This man had spent his whole life begging for scraps of money,, of kindness, of just enough to get by one more day. He didn’t expect to be treated with respect, he didn’t expect anyone to REALLY look at him and see him for who he was- a man created by God, known and loved. He was used to being seen only as a “problem’ - maybe even an eyesore,maybe even a burden to his family and friends because it does say that he was carried to the temple gate every day, I wonder who carried him there and if they left him without water or food for hours or if they stayed close enough to watch and make sure he was not bullied or mistreated by strangers? Did they hover just out of sight so people would have more mercy and give to him, thinking he was all alone? Did they carry him there early in the morning and then pick him back up on the way home from wherever they were? Did he even have a home? Scripture doesn’t tell me all those details but it does tell me what I need to know and what makes my heart dance with joy, and gives me hope.. “Silver or gold, I do not have, but what I do have, I give you, In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk,” Then Peter took this lame man by the right hand and helped him to stand and instantly his feet and ankles became strong. Let that just soak in for a minute or two or even three. The man was touched, he was not simply spoken to, but instead Peter reached out his hand and grasped the hand of the lame man. I am a ‘touchy-feely’ woman, not only because of my hearing loss, or because I am a massage therapist as well as a writer, but also because I was raised by my Mema who used her hands to touch, to soothe, to teach, and to give. Touch was her “love language" and it became mine just as naturally as breathing. It is who I am and what I do so reading these words is like a balm to my soul and it brings tears to my eyes. He helped him up. Isn’t that so simple and yet so very, very sacred and special? Peter helped the lame man up, which means not only did he reach out to take his right hand but his heart led that movement, his eyes noticed that the man needed the help in order to stand and he used his body to stabilize and left that lame man up. Just imagine this for a moment with me- when we take the position to help someone else, we are being humble and kind, yes, but we are also using our body the way it was created to be used- as a vessel of love, a living sacrifice,after all, We are all God’s handiwork, created in Christ Jesus, to do good works which God prepared in advance for us to do. (Ephesians 2:10) How often do we think about others hardships as our opportunity to be a blessing? Not to gloat and to point fingers of judgment or condemnation or to ignore and go about our way He but instead to look at it as if it is a way that God has used to give us a chance to share what He has blessed us with, and to make us more grateful and appreciative of what we do have as well as what we do not have.. That is how I have been looking at things for the last four years as I have walked through this wilderness and stood at my own “Beautiful Gate” - it has changed me from the inside out and I am beginning to see that it is all part of His plans to make me see Him clearer and to see the wonders of His love like never before.. He became strong. He was lame, but then he became strong- instantly, right that second. This is how we need to believe in God’s power and might- fully realizing and expecting that He can change the world on a dime, so to speak. He can do anything in the very second it takes to type this word, or for you to read them , and understand them. In scripture it is written, “Be strong, all you people of the land, declares the L:ord, and work, For I am with you,’declares the Lord Almighty" and there it is- simple as that- we are made to work, and expected to work and once that man’s legs became strong, he jumped up and praised God and I like to think that was a form of work- worshipping with all he had. Sometimes we have to be strong while we wait on the Lord to do what He knows is best even if that means we do not quite understand just yet. I imagine, though, that we are meant to prepare our hearts (Psalms 27;14) as we wait expectantly for what is to come. I also believe that it is important to care for the body we have been given and to strengthen it in all the ways we can while we wait because of what else is written- “but, as for you, be strong and do not give up, for your work will be rewarded,”(2 Chronicles 15:7) The lame man didn’t give up- every single day, he was at the gate called Beautiful , hoping for just enough to get by, but he was also willing to do the next steps when the chance came- he looked up, he extended his hand, he allowed himself to be helped up and then, he jumped for joy and he praised God! He didn’t give up and we should not give up either, They recognized him. “When all the people saw him walking and praising God: they recognized him as the same man who used to sit begging at the temple gate called Beautiful and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him,” (Acts 3:10) I am going to get very real and raw here. Over 4 years ago, I got sick- really sick- with Covid and it has turned into a type of wilderness, a battleground that I didn’t expect but have learned so much through it all. I live and work in a tittle town in Texas that has grown by leaps and bounds over the last several years but it is still small enough that local business owners like myself are known,- sometimes by who we are just as much as who we are not and sometimes by the business we own and the people we are related to and the places we are seen at the most. I was once upon a time that woman that was always at the local gyms and yes, it was gyms- plural- because I would go to the big gym, Legends when I had extra time, and to the smaller Anytime Fitness when time was shorter because it was only two miles away. I loved going to the gyms, and spending hours working out. It was my thing and people knew if I wasn’t at my massage studio, or home, I was at the gym. It was that simple. I took care of my body, sometimes by ignoring my soul- listening to the harsh and fickle voices of condemnation and comparison rather than the voice of my Creator. I rushed from work, to the gym, to home without ever really stopping to rest or to be totally present with those I love and with those that God had placed in my life. O was known, alright, but it was not always the way I wanted to be known.. There is another thing that sets me apart. Something that I was so ashamed of for my entire childhood and all the way up into even now. I have profound hearing loss- I depend on lipreading to understand people.. I lost most of my hearing when I was only five years old and, from the very beginning, I learned to hide it, to pretend I could understand more than I actually did, and not to own up to it or admit it because that would make others think less of me. When I decided to open my own massage studio, Rockwall Body and Soul Massage in 2013, some people told me that I would fail because “it is hard enough for hearing people to make a business succeed and it will be impossible for a half deaf woman to make it” and so I felt like I had to prove myself capable enough, smart enough, strong enough and determined enough to make it, in spite of having a hearing loss. It became something I was always trying to be “enough” of I just aboutI forgot one really important part of this whole process- that I was blessed beyond measure and loved beyond my wildest hopes and dreams and that the God that created me did so knowing full well that I would be half deaf, full of hope, full of creativity and full of possibilities., He had made me to be enough but I had not paid attention to Him- not really, not wholly, but more like an afterthought, I prayed and I read the bible once in a blue moon and I was “good” and I was thoughtful and considerate and loving - to those that were also thoughtful, considerate and loving to me most of the time. I was self centered and not God centered at all. So in August of 2021 when I got Covid, I was already pushing my body to the max. I was already running on fumes- burning the proverbial candle at both ends and I was emotionally and spiritually worn out. Just plain worn out and I was pretending everything was just fine and dandy but it wasn’t…not by a long shot. So when Covid got inside my body, it shook me to the core- my central nervous system went under attack and everything that I had built up fell apart- my legs wouldn’t hold me up so walking was difficult, my vision blurred, my speech became slurred because my lips went numb along the top and the woman I used te be became someone I didn’t even recognize in the mirror. But everyday, God has shown up for me at my very own Beautiful gate in ways I still can not wrap my mind around. My husband and my children have shown me love and patience, and consideration and thoughtfullness in ways that astonish me and humble me and make me ever so grateful. I was once so ashamed of my deafness, only to find it is the very least of my problems. I once thought I could work out and make my body perform only to find out that my body had a mind of its own and I had to let it heal from the inside out. I used to think I had to be pretty enough in order to be loved enough, only to be loved more than ever before when I didn’t look or feel pretty or strong or worthy at all. I have spent the last four years letting go of what I thought I was supposed to be to become what God created me to be and it has not been easy. It has been a spiritual journey through a wilderness where I had to forget the former things in order to reach out and see the new things that He was doing in my life. I needed to become weak so I could see and feel His strength, and I needed to have my vision changed so that I could see His goodness and grace and meet so many people that He has created in His image, That is the purpose and reason for me riding with strangers in Uber almost 1.000 times now. I needed to find out how I was living my life on auto pilot instead of in awe and with praise and gratitude. I needed to lay at the gate of Beautiful and wait for God to move the mountains and break the chains of what was in my body and my soul. This time was essential, just like planting a tree. It takes time for a tree to grow, to establish roots deep enough to withstand storms and drought, and to grow strong and beautiful and able to provide shelter and safety and guidance for others. I have always loved trees but I was more of a wildflower- blooming only for a season-kinda like it says in Job 14:3 -”They spring up like flowers and wither away, like fleeting shadows, they do not endure,’ and God had always meant for me to be set apart as a tree and to claim this deep in my soul and out loud, “ But I am like an olive tree flourishing in the house of God; I trust in God’s unfailing love for ever and ever.” ( Psalms 52;8) By faith in the name of Jesus. That man that they saw and knew was made strong by faith and I believe with all my heart that the name of Jesus will also bring complete healing to me and that I, too, will jump up and walk and be recognized as I give all Glory and praise to God alone for what He h;as done because I have deep faith that is in this scripture, “ Being confident in this; that He who Began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus,” It is Jesus’ name and the faith that comes through him that has completely healed him as you can all see.” (Acts 3:27 Faith, Jesus, Healed. Those three words. Those three truths. I believe. I have faith. I have Jesus. He has healed my soul, He has begun a good work in me - healing my body and my eyes and now, He will complete the healing process and as I go deeper and deeper in my walk with God, out of the wilderness and the dry desert into the new things, I will open up the beautiful gate and I will praise my Savior all the day long. ;14
