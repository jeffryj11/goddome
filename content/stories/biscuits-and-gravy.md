I remember way back when\
biscuits and gravy\
were part of my everyday\
hot as fire\
piled high on my plate

it’s been years and years\
since I’ve sat down\
to biscuits and gravy\
hot as fire\
piled high on my plate

but I remember well\
how good love tasted

💗 *Mema on my mind*\
*Jeanna’ Mead — 7:16 a.m. 4-26-18*
