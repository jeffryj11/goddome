**By Jeanna’ Mead** *A continuation of words written by another…* Sometimes something I read hits me so deeply that I have to write too. This is what I read in a YouVersion devotional: --- ## **The Whispering Spot** *(excerpt from devotional)* When I first toured the Capitol more than two decades ago, our guide revealed a secret that wasn’t really a secret: the whispering spot. He stood on one side of Statuary Hall while our tour group stood on the other side. Then he spoke in a whisper, and sure enough, we could hear the echo of his voice all the way across the room as if he were mere inches away. When I look through the Bible, I see whispering spots everywhere. - For Jacob, it was Bethel. - For Moses, it was a burning bush. - For Samuel, it was the tabernacle at Shiloh. - For David, it was the cave of Adullam. - For Elijah, it was Mount Carmel. - For Daniel, it was an upstairs window facing Jerusalem. - For Jonah, it was the belly of a whale. When I survey Scripture, I see God showing up in strange places, at strange times, in strange ways. And I don’t think anything has changed. God still turns appointments into divine appointments. He still uploads desires, opens doors, and inspires dreams. He still speaks through promptings and people and pain. And just as he did for Moses, He can turn any patch of ground into holy ground. Listening doesn’t happen by default; it happens by design. You have to seek solitude, seek silence. You have to ruthlessly eliminate distractions. You have to turn some voices down or tune them out altogether. Why not turn off the radio and talk to God during your commute? Or fast from social media? Or take a silent retreat? Your whispering spot will be as unique as you are, but you need to find a time and place to hear the still, small voice of God. Do you have a “whispering spot” in your life? If not, where might that spot be? If you do, how has God whispered to you there? --- ## **Here Are My Words. My Whispering Spot.** Last night, my Riven spent the night with me. We dug through treasures — because my hope chest collapsed and I had to take everything out. As we looked through pictures of me as a child holding dolls, I placed my mom’s childhood doll into Riven’s hands… and then Mema’s. I felt a rush of **generational love** sweep over us. Then I showed her the thimble I found years ago underneath my bed — years after Mema danced into glory and after changing beds twice. That tiny thimble was a sign from God: **Remember where you came from. Remember who you are.** He’s been whispering to me. He’s been getting close enough that I can **read His lips**. He keeps placing devotionals and scripture into my hands. He uses anything and everything to get my attention. I just shake my head and say: **“Okay Jesus, what are You telling me?”** And right away: **“Who raised you? Who inspired you? What would Mema do?”** And damn if I don’t start thinking about prune cake, pinto beans, and her open invitation to anyone and everyone. I can make all kinds of excuses and then I hear her whisper: **“So what?”** So what?! There it is. There’s the whisper. - So what if I mess up? - So what if my scars show? - So what if I’m walking without grace? - So what if my home isn’t fancy? - So what if my paint needs another coat? - So what if some people think I’m bat-shit crazy? - So what if my words aren’t always pretty? - So what if people don’t talk to me anymore? - So what if it’s hard to start over? There’s **no reason** to act ugly. And **no reason** not to get up and dance to this song inside my soul. That’s how the Spirit whispers to me: - through music - through memories - through moments - through treasures passed down > **“I’m going to do a new thing. > Do you not perceive it? > I will make streams in the desert > and a way in the wilderness.”** I’m listening. I’m reading His lips. I’m going to do what He’s telling me to do. **So what if it’s crazy?** Jesus made me that way. And God made sure I was raised by a woman who talked sense into me over and over again. Now I have to whisper those stories to Riven… and write them for people like you to read. It’s a whisper in your soul.
